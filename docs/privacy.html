Allnighter

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 27, 2026

What Allnighter collects

Nothing. Allnighter does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your settings, such as the sounds and dimming options, are saved in the app's preferences on your Mac.
  • When the screen is dimmed, your previous brightness level is saved in the app's folder on your Mac so it can be restored. It is deleted as soon as the brightness comes back.

None of this leaves your Mac. Deleting the app removes it.
